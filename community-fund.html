Our focus

Help where it matters.

The Community Fund connects the foundation’s commitment to service with local needs and opportunities.

Disaster relief & recovery

Support for communities facing the disruption and rebuilding that follow severe storms and flooding.

Veterans & military families

Supporting organizations that serve those who have served and the families alongside them.

Community well-being

Community organizations, family health, and revitalization efforts that strengthen places and the people in them.

Disaster recovery is one of the fund’s areas of support.

Past areas of support

Recovery is a long commitment.

The foundation’s previously published work includes Hurricane Helene relief in Western North Carolina, Hurricane Idalia relief, and support for community organizations.

Explore the broader record of support and ask the foundation for details about individual projects.
