At a glance

The scholarship.

The details below describe the previously published scholarship. Final requirements and dates for the 2027 cycle will be confirmed before applications open.

Annual award
$5,000
Renewable
Up to 4 years
Potential total
Up to $20,000

Previously published eligibility

  • U.S. high school senior or current college student
  • Ages 17–24
  • Minimum 3.5 GPA
  • Accepted to a U.S. college or university
  • Demonstrated commitment to service

Priority was given to veterans, children of veterans, and students pursuing service-oriented careers.

Prepare for the next cycle

  1. Review eligibility. Check back for the confirmed 2027 requirements before preparing an application.
  2. Reflect on your service. Think about a moment when service or resilience shaped your perspective.
  3. Wait for application instructions. The opening date, deadline, and submission process will be published here.

Please do not send application documents through the general inquiry form.

Before you apply

Your questions, answered.

Can I apply now?

The next cycle is 2027. Opening and closing dates have not yet been announced. This page will carry the application instructions when they are available.

What happened to the 2026 dates?

The previously published deadline was March 31, 2026, with an award notification date of May 15, 2026. Those dates belong to the past cycle and do not apply to 2027.

What should I know about the essay?

The previous cycle requested a 500–750 word handwritten essay, submitted as a PDF by email separately from the form. The 2027 format and submission method will be confirmed before applications open.

The previous prompt was: “Describe a moment when service or resilience shaped your perspective. How will you use these values to influence the future of your community or the global economy?”

What if I need an accessible submission format?

Contact the foundation to discuss your needs before preparing materials. The foundation can clarify available options and requirements.

Can I ask about renewal or eligibility?

Yes. Send a scholarship question for clarification about award conditions, renewal, or your circumstances.
