An evening with purpose

Come together.
Carry service forward.

Hosted at the Mattis home in North Carolina, Evening Under the Oaks brings friends, community leaders, veterans, and supporters together around a shared table.

The benefit supports the foundation’s scholarship and community work. A limited, invitation-based gathering keeps the evening personal; a shared purpose makes it meaningful.
